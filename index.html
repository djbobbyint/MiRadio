FAQ

Questions people ask before signing up

Is this FM radio? Who can hear me?

It's internet radio, so there's no transmitter, no broadcast licence and no range limit. Anyone in the world with a phone, computer, smart speaker or connected car can listen via your listen page, the embeddable player, or the MiRadio Listen directory.

Do I have to pay anything to start?

No. Every account starts on the Free plan with no card required. You get a real station on air so you can upload music, go live and hear your stream before deciding to upgrade.

Can I really broadcast live from my phone?

Yes, that's the whole idea. Sign in to the MiRadio Broadcaster app on iOS or Android and tap Go Live. AutoDJ lowers the music under your voice and brings it back up when you finish.

Does my plan cover music licences and royalties?

No. As with every streaming host, you're responsible for your own music licences. In the US that usually means SoundExchange for recordings plus ASCAP, BMI and SESAC for songwriting. Other countries have their own collecting societies. If you only play your own or royalty-free music, you may not need any.

Can I use OBS, BUTT, Mixxx or VirtualDJ?

Yes. Any Icecast-compatible encoder connects straight to your station. Your server, mount and password are in the dashboard, along with a setup guide for each tool.

Why not just go live on social media?

A social live stream ends when you stop talking. A radio station plays 24/7 whether you're there or not, listeners don't need an account to tune in, and the audience is yours: your link, your brand, your income.

I'm not technical. Will someone help me?

Yes. Support lives inside your dashboard as a chat, and our team is a message away. Most stations are fully set up in a single sitting.

Can I cancel or change plans any time?

Yes. Upgrades are prorated immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period so your stream never drops. Cancel from the dashboard in one click.
